# Tentative Program

The proposed **one-day workshop** combines invited talks, contributed research, competition highlights, a panel, and interactive poster sessions. The workshop date, venue, session order, and local times will be announced. The table below describes planned session formats, rather than a finalized timetable.

| Session | Planned format | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Invited talks | Six talks, including Q&A | 6 × 30 minutes |
| Original Research contributed talks | Three selected papers | 3 × 10 minutes |
| Position contributed talks | Three selected papers | 3 × 10 minutes |
| Iterative Improvement competition highlights | Two highest-ranked proposals | 2 × 10 minutes |
| AI Credit Assignment competition highlights | Two highest-ranked proposals | 2 × 10 minutes |
| Panel discussion | Four panelists; topic and moderator to be announced | 60 minutes |
| Poster sessions | Interactive discussion of accepted papers and proposals | 2 × 60 minutes |

## Invited and Contributed Talks

Invited speakers will share perspectives on AI for Science across disciplines. The invited talk lineup will be announced. Three submissions from each of the Original Research and Position tracks are planned to be selected for podium presentations.

## Competition Highlights

The two highest-ranked proposals from each of the [proposal competitions]({{ '/iclr27/competition.html' | relative_url }}) are planned to be selected for podium presentations. Cash awards are planned, with sponsorship and award details to be announced.

## Panel Discussion

The proposal lists **Venkat Viswanathan (University of Michigan), Anima Anandkumar (Caltech), Risa Wechsler (Stanford University), and James Zou (Stanford University)** as panelists. The panel topic and moderator will be announced. See the [About page]({{ '/iclr27.html' | relative_url }}) for participant profiles.

## Poster Sessions

Each accepted paper or proposal is planned to be presented in one interactive poster session, creating space for deeper discussion with workshop participants.

## Accessibility and Online Materials

We encourage speakers and presenters to attend in person and plan to facilitate participation for virtual attendees and authors unable to travel. Paper lists, presentation slides, and talks will be released online as they become available, with speakers' consent. Livestream and remote participation details will be announced.

## Post-Workshop Networking Event

An evening networking event is planned to connect scientists and AI researchers beyond the technical program. Partners, location, timing, and registration details will be announced.
