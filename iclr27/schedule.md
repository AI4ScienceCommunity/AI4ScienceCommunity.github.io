# Program

The **one-day workshop** combines invited talks, contributed research, competition highlights, a panel, and interactive poster sessions. The workshop date, venue, and session times are TBA.

| Session | Format | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Invited talks | Six talks, including Q&A | 6 × 30 minutes |
| Original Research contributed talks | Three selected papers | 3 × 10 minutes |
| Position contributed talks | Three selected papers | 3 × 10 minutes |
| Iterative Improvement competition highlights | Two highest-ranked proposals | 2 × 10 minutes |
| AI Credit Assignment competition highlights | Two highest-ranked proposals | 2 × 10 minutes |
| Panel discussion | Topic, moderator, and panelists TBA | 60 minutes |
| Poster sessions | Interactive discussion of accepted papers and proposals | 2 × 60 minutes |

## Invited and Contributed Talks

Invited speakers **Venkat Viswanathan (University of Michigan), Anima Anandkumar (Caltech), Risa Wechsler (Stanford University), and James Zou (Stanford University)** will share perspectives on AI for Science across disciplines. Additional speakers will be announced. See the [About page]({{ '/iclr27.html' | relative_url }}) for speaker profiles. Three submissions from each of the Original Research and Position tracks will be selected for podium presentations.

## Competition Highlights

The two highest-ranked proposals from each of the [proposal competitions]({{ '/iclr27/competition.html' | relative_url }}) will be selected for podium presentations. Sponsors and cash award amounts are TBA.

## Panel Discussion

The panel topic, moderator, and panelists are TBA.

## Poster Sessions

Each accepted paper or proposal will be presented in one interactive poster session, creating space for deeper discussion with workshop participants.

## Accessibility and Online Materials

We encourage speakers and presenters to attend in person and will facilitate participation for virtual attendees and authors unable to travel. Paper lists, presentation slides, and talks will be released online as they become available, with speakers' consent. Livestream and remote participation details will be announced.

## Post-Workshop Networking Event

An evening networking event will connect scientists and AI researchers beyond the technical program. Partners, location, timing, and registration details will be announced.
